# Stationfall: how it was made

This is the history of a web remake of Infocom's *Stationfall* (1987), faithful to the original and playable by mouse or keyboard, with painted rooms. The sponsor sets direction and decides at the gates; Norm (project lead and developer) and Greg (designer and artist) are AI agents who do the work and talk through a mailbox. The pages record what was decided, what went wrong and what changed, because the process is meant to be shown as well as the game.

- [How to win](walkthrough.md): the critical path from the Duffy to the Factory, as the original scores it, 80 of 80.
- [Journal](journal.md): one entry per milestone, release or playtest round.
- [Timeline](timeline.md): the milestones, one line each.
- [Tools](tools.md): the tools the project built or carried over.
- [How we work](how-we-work.md): the mailbox between Norm and Greg, and how efficient we are, in charts generated from the record.
  - [The contract, version by version](contract-history.md): each version of the Norm–Greg contract, what it changed, why, and whether it helped.
  - [The contract's text, every version](contract-versions.md): the contract itself at each version, and what each changed from the one before, line by line.
- [How we tested playability](playtesting.md): the blind playtests, from the segmented rounds to the full runs: the method, every round's results, and the progress, outcome and friction charts.
  - [The playtesters' leaderboard](playtest-leaderboard.md): every full run ranked by score, frustration, blocks and variety of feedback, and how much of the friction comes from the persona.
  - [Playtests, round by round](playtests.md): every blind playtest as the testers left it: each session's diary, friction rows and outcome, with its round's triage and stats.
- [Where we left the original](canon-deviations.md): the register of deliberate deviations, and what was declined.
